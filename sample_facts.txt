Project Aethelgard is a top-secret deep-space research mission code-named "Starshade".
It was launched on September 12, 2028, from the Svalbard Rocket Range.
The principal investigator is Dr. Elena Vance.
The primary payload is a prototype quantum-entangled sensor that operates at 12.4 microkelvin.
The target destination is the L2 Lagrange point.
